Llion Jones is a Welsh computer scientist and entrepreneur, best known as a co-author of the groundbreaking 2017 research paper “Attention Is All You Need,” which introduced the Transformer architecture that underpins many modern artificial intelligence systems. After spending over a decade at Google, where he worked on natural language processing, Llion co-founded Sakana AI in 2023. Based in Tokyo, he currently serves as the company’s Chief Technology Officer.
