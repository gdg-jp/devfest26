Theme

For beginners

Meet the Vibes

Pick up AI and make something.

No code needed. Sessions on the fun of turning an idea into something real with AI beside you — from making everyday work easier to building a small app — with tips you can try tomorrow.

Good for you if you

  • New to AI
  • Rarely write code
  • Want to build something
Intermediate & up

Beyond the Vibes

Past the vibes, into design and practice.

Beyond vibe coding: engineers from the field dig into designing products with AI inside, and what it takes for a team to ship with it.

Good for you if you

  • Write code every day
  • Building AI into a product
  • Want your team to ship faster

FAQ

Is there a fee?

No, it is free, and every attendee gets a gift.

I'm not an engineer. Can I still come?

Yes. Designers, PMs, people in business — whatever your role, you are welcome. If you don't write code, try the Meet the Vibes sessions, where you get to make things with AI.

Can students attend?

Yes, whatever your year or field. If you are under 18, please get a parent's or guardian's consent before you register.

Is there support in English?

Yes, there is support in English. Details will be posted on this page as they are settled.

When does registration close?

At 18:00 on November 29, unless a ticket type fills up first — all are first come, first served.

A safe place for everyone

GDG events are harassment-free. Please follow the Code of Conduct at the venue and when posting online.
